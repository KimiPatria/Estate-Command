-- why this could be wrong --
  * Context below TimesFM's own 32-point minimum on most folds: the
    walk-forward starts at 6 months of history. 3 variant(s) had
    fewer matched-context folds than total folds.
  * The shippable backend (2.5) is univariate-only on Windows -- its
    XReg covariate path needs jax[cuda], which has no Windows wheels.
  * Zero-shot: the model has no palm-agronomy signal. The incumbent
    uses pruning man-days at lag 1, pruning qty at lag 5, fertiliser
    at lag 3 and rainfall at flowering 5-7 months prior.
  * Single estate, ~30 folds. A 90% coverage estimate has ~7pp
    standard error at this fold count; treat coverage claims loosely.
